# Dataset notes: seams and deliberate gaps
All Pearlz Home Systems content is fictional and authored for this assignment. No real-company policy is copied.

## Cross-document seams
1. Warranty Policy describes eligible components; Service and Maintenance Agreement describes claim triage, visits and parts. A leak/claim question requires both.
2. Shipping Policy gives regional timelines; Customer Service Handbook defines ticket escalation and customer contact handling.
3. Product Manual (manual details are summarised in Troubleshooting and Installation guides for this compact build) and Product Changelog together resolve the firmware 2.4 E-03 beep-pattern change.

## Deliberate knowledge gaps
- Student discounts are not specified.
- NSF certification status is not specified.
- Instalment financing is not specified.
- International shipping is not offered; no exception workflow is documented.
- Compensation for consequential household property damage is not promised.

## Consistency decisions
AquaPearl 500 Pro standard warranty is 24 months from invoice date. Standard return request window is 14 calendar days from delivery. Free standard shipping threshold is PKR 15,000. Remote delivery is estimated at 5-7 working days. The assistant must refuse unsupported claims rather than infer answers.
