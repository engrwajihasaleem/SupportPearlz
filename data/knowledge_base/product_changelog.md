# Product Changelog and Release Notes
Version 1.0 | Updated 6 November 2026
## Firmware 2.2 — 10 May 2026
Improved filter-life estimate stability for AquaPearl 500 Pro. No alert-code definitions changed in this release.
## Firmware 2.3 — 18 July 2026
Improved mobile pairing reliability and added a clearer Bluetooth permission prompt. Existing alert-code definitions remained unchanged.
## Firmware 2.4 — 15 September 2026
Changed the audible pattern for E-03 on AquaPearl 500 Pro from one long beep to three short beeps. The underlying meaning remains sediment pre-filter saturation. Customers with firmware 2.4 should follow the troubleshooting guide's three-beep instruction. The older product manual may describe the pre-2.4 sound pattern; use this changelog to interpret the sound pattern, and the troubleshooting guide for the safe action.
## Known limitations
This changelog does not state whether any filter has third-party NSF certification. It does not provide a future firmware release date.
