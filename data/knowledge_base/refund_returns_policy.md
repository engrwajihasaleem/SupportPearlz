# Refund and Returns Policy
Version 1.0 | Effective 1 October 2026
## 1. Return window
A customer may request a standard return within 14 calendar days after delivery. The request must be submitted through the account portal or by contacting support. The 14-day period is measured from confirmed delivery, not order placement.
## 2. Condition requirements
Unopened products in original sealed packaging are eligible for a standard return after inspection. Opened appliances may be accepted only if complete, undamaged, clean, and accompanied by all accessories; a 10% restocking fee may apply. Products with missing accessories, signs of installation, water exposure, or customer-caused damage may be rejected or assessed for a partial refund. Hygiene-related replacement cartridges that have been opened are non-returnable unless defective on arrival.
## 3. Shipping costs
For a change-of-mind return of an unopened item, the customer pays return shipping. If Pearlz shipped the wrong SKU or the item arrived damaged, Pearlz arranges return collection without charge after evidence is reviewed. Remote-area collection may take longer than metro collection.
## 4. Refund timing
After the returned item passes inspection, card refunds are submitted within 5-7 business days; bank transfer refunds are submitted within 7-10 business days; cash-on-delivery refunds are issued by bank transfer within 10 business days after verified bank details are received. The bank or payment provider may take additional time to post the credit.
## 5. Non-refundable items and fees
Opened consumable cartridges, gift cards, installation labour already delivered, and shipping fees on a change-of-mind return are not refundable. A restocking fee may apply to an opened appliance accepted under section 2. Promotional bundles must be returned as a complete set; if an item is retained, the bundle discount is recalculated at the standard item price.
## 6. Return, replacement and warranty repair
A return reverses the purchase after inspection. A replacement exchanges an eligible item for the same or an approved equivalent SKU. A warranty repair is a service remedy for a covered manufacturing defect and follows the warranty policy. These are different processes; a warranty claim is not automatically a refund.
## 7. Partial refunds
If an eligible return is incomplete but can be resold after inspection, Pearlz may offer a partial refund reflecting missing accessories or documented condition. The exact amount is determined after inspection and communicated in writing. No fixed percentage is promised except the stated 10% restocking fee where applicable.
## 8. Missing information
This policy does not specify student discounts, instalment financing, or compensation for damage to a customer's home. Customers must contact support for individual review; the assistant must not guess or promise an outcome.
