# Customer Service Handbook
Version 1.0 | Internal service process summary
## 1. Support hours and channels
Customer support operates Monday to Saturday, 09:00-18:00 Pakistan Standard Time, excluding public holidays. Customers may use the online contact form, email support@pearlzhome.example or the WhatsApp support link shown in their account. The assignment contact is fictional.
## 2. Ticket priorities
Priority 1: safety issue such as smoke, electric shock, uncontrolled leak or electrical smell. Advise the customer to stop use, isolate power only if safe, close the water inlet where relevant, and escalate immediately to the duty lead. Never instruct a customer to open the housing.
Priority 2: purifier unavailable, repeated fault code, or significant water-flow interruption. Acknowledge within one business day and route to technical support.
Priority 3: routine filter, order, delivery or account question. Acknowledge within two business days.
## 3. Complaint handling
Listen without arguing, restate the issue, collect only necessary order and product details, check the current approved policy, and explain the next step. Do not promise a refund, replacement, compensation, delivery exception or warranty approval before the relevant team has reviewed the case.
## 4. Compensation authority
Front-line agents may not approve cash compensation or waive policy fees. A shift lead may review a documented service recovery request, but no monetary limit or automatic entitlement is defined in this handbook. Escalate requests for property-damage compensation to the service manager.
## 5. Tone
Use concise, respectful language. Acknowledge inconvenience without admitting fault before investigation. Explain known facts, separate estimates from guarantees, and state when documentation does not answer a question. Never invent contact channels, ticket numbers or actions.
