# Service and Maintenance Agreement
Version 1.0 | Service operations
## 1. Plans
Basic Care is included during the standard warranty period and provides remote troubleshooting plus one scheduled inspection when a covered fault is accepted. Plus Care costs PKR 4,500 per year and includes one preventive visit and priority booking. Complete Care costs PKR 8,500 per year and includes two preventive visits, priority booking and labour for listed routine maintenance. Replacement consumables are billed separately under all tiers.
## 2. Visit frequency and response targets
Basic Care: remote response within two business days; on-site appointment target within five business days after diagnosis where coverage and technician capacity permit. Plus Care: remote response within one business day and appointment target within three business days. Complete Care: remote response within four business hours and appointment target within two business days. These are service targets, not guaranteed repair completion times; remote destinations may require additional travel time.
## 3. Parts and labour
Covered warranty components are supplied according to the warranty policy after inspection. Filters, membranes, accidental damage parts, unauthorised-repair damage and out-of-warranty components are chargeable. Labour for approved routine maintenance is included in Complete Care; parts remain separately chargeable unless expressly listed as included.
## 4. Claim and visit procedure
The customer provides invoice number, serial number, fault description and safe photographs or video. Support creates a ticket, performs remote triage, checks warranty eligibility and schedules a visit. The technician records diagnosis, parts used and the customer's acknowledgement. The service desk then communicates the resolution and any charge before non-covered work proceeds.
## 5. Cancellation
Annual plans may be cancelled within seven days of purchase if no visit or priority service has been used. After that period, cancellation is allowed but fees already used are deducted. Any eligible pro-rata balance is calculated by the service desk and returned to the original payment method where possible. No automatic full refund is promised.
## 6. Cross-document claim rule
The warranty policy defines which component faults may be covered; this agreement defines how a claim is triaged and scheduled. Both documents must be read together. Neither document promises reimbursement for damage to cabinets, floors or other property.
