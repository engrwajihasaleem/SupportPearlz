# Troubleshooting Guide
Version 1.0 | Support engineering
## 1. Safety first
Disconnect power before inspecting external connections. Never open the housing, bypass a sensor, handle wet electrical parts, or attempt pump repair. Stop use and contact support immediately if you smell burning, see smoke, receive an electric shock, or observe a cracked pressurised water line.
## 2. Symptom table
| Customer symptom | Likely cause | Safe action |
|---|---|---|
| No power or display | Loose adapter or outlet issue | Check the supplied adapter connection and a known working dry outlet; do not use a damaged cable. |
| Low water flow | Saturated pre-filter or low inlet pressure | Check inlet valve is open; inspect filter replacement date. |
| Three short beeps | E-03 sediment pre-filter saturation | Replace the sediment pre-filter; hold RESET for five seconds. |
| One long beep | E-01 startup self-test issue | Power-cycle once; if repeated, contact support. |
| Two beeps | E-02 inlet pressure below range | Check the inlet valve; do not adjust building plumbing without a plumber. |
| Four beeps | E-04 tank fill timeout | Check water supply and contact support if it repeats. |
| Five beeps | E-05 leak sensor alert | Turn off inlet water and power; do not restart until inspected. |
| Six beeps | E-06 pump temperature alert | Switch off for 30 minutes; if recurring, request service. |
| Seven beeps | E-07 sensor communication fault | Do not open the unit; contact support. |
| Eight beeps | E-08 internal electronics fault | Disconnect power and arrange service. |
| Blinking red LED | Active warning or filter alert | Read the displayed code and consult this guide. |
| Bad taste after filter change | Initial carbon fines or incomplete flush | Run the first-use flush for the time in the manual; if taste persists, stop drinking and contact support. |
| Water leaking at base | Loose external tubing or internal leak | Close inlet valve, disconnect power if safe, and contact support. Do not open casing. |
| Pump runs continuously | Float or flow sensor fault | Close inlet valve and contact support; do not bypass sensors. |
| High TDS reading | Feed-water variation or sensor calibration | Compare source-water reading; request service if purifier output remains outside documented range. |
| Noisy pump | Air in line or pump fault | Check inlet valve and perform the documented flush; repeated noise requires service. |
| Mobile app will not pair | Bluetooth permission or network mismatch | Enable Bluetooth and local-network permission; restart app and pairing mode. |
| Water does not stop filling | Valve or sensor issue | Close inlet valve immediately and contact support. |
## 3. Escalation
E-05, E-07, E-08, electrical smell, smoke, shock, persistent base leakage, and continuous uncontrolled filling require immediate shutdown and human support. Customers must not open the housing or attempt internal repairs.
