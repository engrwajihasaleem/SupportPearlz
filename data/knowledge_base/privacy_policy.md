# Pearlz Privacy Policy
Version 1.0 | Effective 1 October 2026
## 1. Information collected
When a customer places an order, Pearlz collects name, delivery address, contact number, email address, order details, payment status and invoice information. Payment-card credentials are handled by the payment processor; Pearlz does not store full card numbers or security codes.
## 2. Mobile application data
If a customer pairs a purifier with the mobile application, the app processes account email, device identifier, pairing status, firmware version, filter-life estimates, alert history and, when the customer enables the feature, water-usage telemetry. The app does not collect audio recordings through the purifier.
## 3. Purpose
Data is used to fulfil orders, provide warranty and service support, send delivery notifications, maintain device connectivity, estimate filter life, diagnose reported faults, and improve reliability. Marketing messages require the customer's communication preference.
## 4. Sharing
Order name, address and contact number are shared with the selected courier for delivery. Payment status and transaction references are shared with the payment processor. Service technicians receive the minimum contact and product details needed to complete an authorised visit. Pearlz does not sell customer personal data.
## 5. Retention
Order and invoice records are retained for seven years for accounting and dispute handling. Support tickets are retained for three years after closure. Mobile telemetry is retained for 12 months, then aggregated or deleted. Account details remain until the account is closed, subject to records that must be retained by law.
## 6. Customer rights
Customers may request access, correction, export or deletion of eligible personal data by emailing support@pearlzhome.example with the subject “Privacy Request”. Pearlz may request identity verification before acting. Some transaction records cannot be deleted while a legal retention obligation applies.
## 7. Cookies
The online store uses essential session cookies for login, cart and checkout. Optional analytics cookies are used only where enabled by the site's consent controls. Blocking essential cookies may prevent checkout from working.
## 8. Contact and limitations
Privacy requests are handled by the privacy desk through support@pearlzhome.example. This policy does not specify a third-party certification status for any filter, nor does it promise a fixed response time for deletion requests.
