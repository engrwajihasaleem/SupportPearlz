# Customer Frequently Asked Questions
Version 1.0 | Customer language reference
## Ordering and delivery
### When will my parcel arrive?
After dispatch, metro locations normally take 1-3 working days, secondary cities 3-5, and remote destinations 5-7. The tracking page may take 12 hours to update.
### Can you send my order tomorrow?
Next-working-day delivery is only shown for eligible postcodes in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad when checkout conditions are met. It is not offered for remote areas such as Skardu.
### Is shipping free?
Standard shipping is free on orders of PKR 15,000 or more. The checkout page shows charges for smaller orders.
### I missed the courier. What now?
The courier normally tries twice. After the second attempt, the parcel may remain at the depot for up to three working days. Contact support with the tracking number.
## Product and filters
### Why is my purifier beeping three times?
Three short beeps indicate E-03, a saturated sediment pre-filter. Replace the sediment pre-filter and hold RESET for five seconds.
### Do I need to flush a new filter?
Yes. The installation guide requires a 10-minute initial flush and disposal of the first full tank.
### Can I wash a disposable cartridge?
No washing or reuse instruction is provided for disposable cartridges. Replace them according to the maintenance schedule in the product manual.
### Why does water taste different after a filter change?
A brief change may occur during the initial flush. Run the prescribed flush; if taste persists, stop drinking and contact support.
## Warranty and returns
### Does my warranty start when the courier arrives?
No. It begins on the invoice purchase date.
### Are filters covered?
Replacement cartridges and other consumables are excluded from the standard purifier warranty.
### Can I return an opened purifier?
It may be accepted within 14 calendar days after delivery if complete, undamaged and clean. A 10% restocking fee may apply after inspection.
### Who pays return shipping?
For a change-of-mind return of an unopened item, the customer pays. Pearlz arranges collection without charge for a wrong SKU or verified transit damage.
### How long does a refund take?
After inspection, card refunds are submitted in 5-7 business days, bank transfers in 7-10, and COD refunds within 10 business days after verified bank details.
### Does warranty cover a cracked case?
Impact damage is excluded. A technician must inspect the product before a coverage decision.
### Can I transfer the warranty to another owner?
No. Warranty is not transferable on resale.
## Installation and maintenance
### Can I install it myself?
You may follow the supplied guide if the site meets its requirements, but do not open or modify the purifier. Plumbing work should be done by a qualified plumber.
### What if my water pressure is too high?
The approved inlet range is 15-60 PSI. Ask a qualified plumber to correct pressure before connecting the unit.
### Is professional installation available?
Standard authorised installation is PKR 2,500 in supported metro areas; additional plumbing or electrical work is excluded.
## App and privacy
### What information does the app collect?
The app processes account email, device identifier, pairing status, filter-life estimates and user-enabled water-usage telemetry. See the privacy policy for retention and controls.
### Can I delete my app data?
Submit a privacy request to support@pearlzhome.example. Identity verification may be required.
## Not documented
The knowledge base does not specify student discounts, instalment financing, NSF certification, international shipping, or compensation for household property damage. The assistant must not infer answers to these questions.
