# Shipping and Delivery Policy
Version 1.0 | Effective 1 October 2026
## 1. Dispatch
Orders paid and confirmed before 12:00 noon Pakistan Standard Time on a working day are normally prepared for same-day dispatch. Orders confirmed after noon, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed on the next working day. Dispatch is not the same as delivery.
## 2. Delivery regions
Metro tier: Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad/Rawalpindi, normally 1-3 working days after dispatch. Secondary-city tier: Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Hyderabad, Gujranwala and Sialkot, normally 3-5 working days. Remote tier: Skardu, Gilgit, Chitral, interior Balochistan and other remote service postcodes, normally 5-7 working days. Timelines are estimates and may change due to weather, road access or courier disruption.
## 3. Express delivery
Next-working-day delivery is offered only for eligible postcodes in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad when the order is confirmed before 10:00 a.m. and the express option is displayed at checkout. Express service is not offered for Skardu or other remote-tier postcodes. The documentation does not define a purchasable exception for remote destinations.
## 4. Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free for orders of PKR 15,000 or more. Orders below that threshold show the applicable charge at checkout. Express delivery, when available, has a separate charge displayed before payment.
## 5. Fragile handling
Purifiers are shipped upright in protective packaging. Customers should photograph visible carton damage before opening and report transit damage within 48 hours of delivery. Keep the packaging until the support team confirms that inspection is complete.
## 6. Failed delivery
The courier normally makes two delivery attempts and records the reason for each failed attempt. After the second failed attempt, the parcel may be held at the local courier depot for up to three working days before return to sender. Contact support promptly with the tracking number to request guidance. A redelivery fee may apply if the address or phone number supplied was incorrect.
## 7. Tracking and international orders
A tracking link is sent after dispatch. Tracking events may take up to 12 hours to appear. International shipping is not currently offered through the Pearlz online store. The policy does not state customs or export arrangements for individual requests.
