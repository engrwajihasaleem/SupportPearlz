# Pearlz Warranty Policy
Version 1.0 | Effective 1 October 2026
## 1. Warranty periods
AquaPearl 300 and AquaPearl 500 Pro purifiers have a limited 24-month warranty. AeroPearl Mini has a 18-month warranty. The period begins on the invoice purchase date, not the delivery date. Customers must retain the original invoice or an approved digital invoice.
## 2. Covered components
During the applicable period, Pearlz covers manufacturing defects in the purifier outer body, internal pump, control electronics, and (for AeroPearl Mini) fan motor. Covered remedies are repair or replacement of the defective component at Pearlz's discretion. The policy does not promise cash compensation for consequential property damage.
## 3. Exclusions
Filter cartridges, membranes, mineralisation media, batteries, and other consumables are excluded. Accidental drops, cracked housings caused by impact, flooding, use outside the specified water-pressure range, water-quality damage caused by corrosive feed water, commercial use of household models, unauthorised opening or repair, altered serial numbers, and failure to follow maintenance instructions are excluded.
### 3.2 Consumables
Replacement filters are consumables and are not covered by the standard purifier warranty, even if a filter becomes saturated earlier than expected. A manufacturing defect in a sealed, unused cartridge may be reviewed under the 7-day delivery-damage process, but this is not an extension of the purifier warranty.
## 4. Claim procedure
Contact customer support with invoice number, serial number, product photographs, a short description, and a safe video of the fault if possible. Do not open the purifier. The service team creates a ticket and arranges diagnosis. The detailed visit scheduling, labour and parts process is governed by the Service and Maintenance Agreement. A technician must inspect the unit before coverage is confirmed.
## 5. Resale and discontinued products
Warranty is not transferable to a second owner. AquaPearl 200 discontinued units retain the remaining original warranty only; no new warranty period begins on resale. AeroPearl Classic units purchased before discontinuation retain the original 12-month term stated on their invoice.
## 6. Extended cover
AquaPearl 500 Pro customers may purchase a 12-month extension within 30 days of the original warranty expiry. The extension covers the same eligible purifier components, not filters, accidental damage or unauthorised repairs. Extension availability must be confirmed by support; it is not automatic.
## 7. Customer responsibilities
Use the supplied power adapter, maintain the inlet pre-filter, keep ventilation clear, and use the product within the installation guide's pressure range. Keep proof of purchase and allow a trained technician to inspect a reported defect. Warranty decisions are made after inspection and written ticket review.
