# Installation Guide
Version 1.0 | AquaPearl household systems
## 1. Before installation
Confirm a stable, level mounting surface, a grounded 220-240 V AC socket, a reachable inlet shutoff valve, and at least 10 cm ventilation clearance on both sides. The approved inlet water pressure range is 15-60 PSI. If pressure is outside this range, consult a qualified plumber before connecting the purifier. Do not connect hot-water lines.
## 2. Wall-mount installation
Use the supplied mounting template, level, drill, suitable wall anchors and screws rated for the wall material. Mark the holes, verify there are no concealed pipes or cables, drill and fit anchors, mount the bracket, and confirm it is level. Lift the purifier with two people and engage the bracket locks. Do not mount on a weak partition or damaged plasterboard without structural support.
## 3. Under-sink installation
Choose a dry, ventilated cabinet with access for filter replacement. Keep the power adapter away from possible leaks. Install the inlet tee and shutoff valve using a qualified plumber where required. Route tubing without sharp bends and secure it away from hot surfaces. Check every connection for leaks before energising the unit.
## 4. First-run flush
Open the inlet valve slowly and inspect all joints. Connect power only after the area is dry. Run the initial flush for 10 minutes and discard the first full tank. This removes harmless carbon fines and air from the new filter path. Do not drink the first flush water. If leakage or abnormal noise occurs, stop and contact support.
## 5. Professional installation
Pearlz-authorised installation is available in supported metro areas for PKR 2,500 per standard installation visit. The price excludes plumbing modifications, new electrical sockets, cabinetry work and additional tubing. Availability and appointment slots are confirmed by the service team.
## 6. Warranty precautions
Do not drill into the purifier housing, modify wiring, use non-approved adapters, bypass the inlet shutoff, or permit an unauthorised person to open the unit. Damage caused by unauthorised modification or installation outside the specified pressure range may be excluded under the warranty policy.
